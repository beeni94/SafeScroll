# SafeScroll 🛡️

Let’s be honest — we’ve all been there. You open a tab to check something quickly, and an hour later you’re trapped in an endless loop of shorts, reels, and brain-rot content. 

I built **SafeScroll** out of pure necessity. It’s a lightweight Chrome extension created to cut through the digital noise, curb doomscrolling, and help us regain control over our online screen time.

---

### 💡 What It Does

- **Filters Out Distractions:** Automatically hides or blurs low-value, addictive content while you browse.
- **Promotes Mindful Browsing:** Keeps you aware of your scrolling habits so you don’t lose hours aimlessly.
- **Dead Simple Controls:** No complicated menus. Toggle protection on or off whenever you need a quick break.
- **Lightweight:** Runs quietly in the background without hogging browser performance or memory.

---

### 🚀 Quick Setup (Chrome Developer Mode)

Want to try it out on your machine?

1. Clone or download this repo to your computer.
2. Enable Developer Mode in Chrome (`chrome://extensions/`).
3. Click "Load unpacked" and select the extension folder.
4. Set your preferences and test it out!
